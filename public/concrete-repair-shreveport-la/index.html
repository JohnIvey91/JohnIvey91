    Concrete Repair in Shreveport, LA

    Cracked, sunken, or damaged concrete is more than just an eyesore. It creates trip hazards, drainage problems, and accelerates deterioration. SC Steel & Concrete provides expert concrete repair services in Shreveport, Bossier City, and across Northwest Louisiana. We identify the root cause of the damage, recommend the most effective repair method, and execute repairs that last.

    Cracked or Damaged Concrete? We Can Fix It.

    Not every damaged slab needs to be torn out and replaced. SC Steel & Concrete identifies what's causing the problem, tells you straight whether repair or replacement makes more sense, and does the work so it holds.

    Why Concrete Fails in Northwest Louisiana

    Northwest Louisiana's expansive clay soil is the root cause of most concrete problems in the region. Clay swells when wet and contracts when dry, constantly shifting the slabs above it.

    Heavy rainfall erodes base material over time, leaving voids beneath the concrete. Those voids cause sections to sink or crack under load. Poor drainage accelerates the whole process.

    We find the cause before we fix the surface. A patch on top of an unresolved problem will fail again, and we won't sell you that.

    Repair or Replace?

    This answer depends on how extensive the damage is, whether the base is still sound, and what the surface is used for.

    Repair Usually Makes Sense When

    • Cracks are isolated and the surrounding concrete is structurally sound
    • Settlement is limited to specific sections that can be lifted and stabilized
    • The surface has spalling or damage but the base beneath is solid
    • The repair area is small relative to the overall slab

    Replacement Usually Makes Sense When

    • Cracking is widespread and the slab has lost structural integrity
    • The base has failed and cannot support a repaired surface
    • Settlement is severe throughout and lifting costs approach replacement costs
    • Concrete was poured too thin or without proper base preparation

    We'll walk you through this decision at the site visit and give you a straight answer either way.

    What We Repair

    Driveway Crack Repair

    Driveway cracks develop from soil settling, tree root intrusion, and wear from vehicle traffic. We evaluate each crack to determine whether it's stable or actively widening before choosing a repair method.

    Stable cracks in otherwise sound concrete get flexible fillers that accommodate minor movement and block water intrusion. Wider cracks or those caused by settling get the underlying support addressed first. Patching the surface without fixing the cause guarantees the crack returns.

    Sunken Concrete Lifting

    Sunken sections create trip hazards, drainage problems, and accelerated deterioration. Where the base is still sound, we raise sunken concrete back to proper grade by filling voids beneath the slab with no tear-out required.

    Depending on the situation, we use mudjacking or polyurethane foam injection. Most lifting projects are completed in a single day with the concrete ready for use within hours.

    Concrete Resurfacing

    Spalling, pitting, staining, and rough surfaces get worse over time and become slippery when wet. Concrete resurfacing applies a new surface layer over existing concrete, restoring its appearance and durability at a fraction of replacement cost.

    Resurfacing can be finished to match existing concrete or upgraded with decorative options including color, texture, or stamped patterns.

    Sidewalk and Walkway Repair

    Sidewalks and walkways carry the added concern of trip hazards from uneven sections. We repair cracked, sunken, or damaged walkways to restore safe, attractive pathways, addressing trip hazards, maintaining proper drainage, and matching existing concrete appearance. Where sections are badly damaged, we replace only the affected panels rather than the entire walkway.

    Patio Crack Repair and Restoration

    Patios take constant exposure to weather, foot traffic, and furniture loads. Cracks let water in, which leads to further damage through soil erosion beneath the slab. We repair patio cracks, address settling, and restore both function and appearance. For patios with extensive surface damage, decorative overlays or resurfacing provide a cost-effective alternative to full replacement.

    Expansion Joint Repair

    When joint material deteriorates, water gets in, edges crack, and weeds take hold. Replacing deteriorated joint material is a straightforward repair that protects slab edges and extends the life of the surrounding concrete.
