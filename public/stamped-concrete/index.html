    STAMPED CONCRETE

    Plain concrete gets the job done. Stamped concrete does that and looks good doing it. If you have been thinking about a new patio, a driveway upgrade, or a pool deck that stands out, stamped concrete gives you the look of stone or brick at a fraction of the cost. SC Steel & Concrete Professionals pours and stamps decorative concrete for homes across Northwest Louisiana, with the same structural standards we bring to every project.

    Where Stamped Concrete Works Best

    Stamped concrete is a strong choice anywhere you want a finished, polished look that holds up to outdoor conditions. Common applications include:

    • Patios and outdoor living spaces
    • Driveways and parking pads
    • Pool decks and walkways
    • Pool decks and walkways
    • Covered porches and porch extensions

    If you can picture it as a concrete surface, there is a good chance we can stamp it.

    Patterns and Colors

    The pattern and color you choose determine how the finished surface looks and how well it fits your home. We walk every customer through the options before work begins so there are no surprises on pour day.

    Some of the most requested patterns in our area include:

    • Appian Running Cobble

    Color Options

    Color is where stamped concrete either looks realistic or looks cheap. We use integral color mixed into the concrete itself, color hardener applied to the surface for added depth, and a release agent during stamping that creates natural-looking variation between the high and low points of the pattern. The result is a surface that reads like real stone or brick rather than painted concrete.

    We bring physical samples to every consultation so you can see colors in natural light before you commit.

    Built for Louisiana Conditions

    Stamped concrete has a reputation in some markets for cracking or fading within a few years. That reputation usually comes from poor installation, not from the material itself. In Louisiana, the two biggest risks are expansive clay soil movement and UV exposure from a long, intense summer.

    We address both. Every decorative slab we pour includes our SteelX reinforcement system as a standard part of the job. Control joints are placed to manage cracking where it is least visible. And we apply a high-quality UV-resistant sealer after the concrete cures that protects the color and surface texture for years.

    Stamped Concrete vs. Pavers and Natural Stone

    A lot of homeowners consider pavers or natural stone before landing on stamped concrete. Here is a straightforward comparison:

    Cost

    Stamped concrete typically runs significantly less per square foot than natural stone and less than most paver installations when you factor in base prep and labor. For larger surfaces like driveways or big patios, that difference adds up fast.

    Maintenance

    Pavers shift over time. Individual pieces settle, gaps open up, and weeds find their way through the joints. Natural stone can crack and is difficult to match if a section needs to be replaced. Stamped concrete is a single continuous surface. It needs periodic resealing but does not have the joint maintenance issues that come with segmented materials.

    Durability

    Properly installed and sealed stamped concrete holds up well in our climate. It handles the heat, the rain, and the soil movement better than pavers when it is reinforced correctly. The key word is correctly. That is where installation quality matters.

    Sealing and Long-Term Care

    We seal every stamped concrete surface we install after the concrete has had time to cure. That sealer protects against staining, slows UV fading, and helps water shed off the surface rather than soaking in. We recommend resealing every 2-3 years to maintain that protection. The good news is that resealing is a simple process that does not require any special equipment. You can do it yourself with a roller or sprayer, or we can handle it for you if you want to make sure it gets done right.
