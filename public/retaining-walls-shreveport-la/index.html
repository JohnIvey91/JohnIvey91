    Retaining Walls in Shreveport, LA

    Retaining walls are more than just a landscaping feature. They protect your property from erosion, stabilize slopes, and create usable outdoor space. SC Steel & Concrete builds retaining walls that hold in Shreveport, Bossier City, and across Northwest Louisiana. We use Redi-Rock systems for their strength, durability, and design flexibility, and we engineer every wall to handle our soil conditions and rainfall patterns.

    When Your Property Needs a Retaining Wall

    Retaining walls are necessary when you have a slope that is causing erosion, drainage problems, or unusable outdoor space. If you have a hillside that is washing out during heavy rains, a retaining wall can stabilize the soil and prevent further damage. If you want to create a level area for a patio, garden, or play space, a retaining wall can hold back the soil and create that flat surface. If you have an existing wall that is leaning or failing, we can replace it with a properly engineered solution.

    We also build retaining walls for commercial properties, including parking lot walls, slope stabilization for building sites, and erosion control structures. If you have a project that requires a retaining wall, contact us for a free site visit and estimate.

    Why Retaining Walls Fail

    Retaining walls fail when they aren't designed and built for the load they are carrying. Water is the most common culprit. If a wall doesn't have a proper drainage plan, water builds up behind it and creates hydrostatic pressure that pushes the wall out of position over time. Soil movement is another factor. Shreveport's clay-heavy soil expands when wet and contracts when dry, putting constant pressure on any structure built on top of it. A wall that isn't engineered to handle those forces will lean, crack, or collapse.

    Cutting corners on footings, using the wrong materials, or skipping reinforcement can also lead to failure. A wall that isn't built on a solid footing or doesn't have the right thickness and reinforcement for the height and load will show signs of distress within a few years. We don't take those shortcuts, and we don't recommend them to our customers. A retaining wall is an investment in your property, and it deserves to be built right.

    How We Build Retaining Walls

    Drainage Planning That Protects Your Investment

    A retaining wall without a drainage plan is a wall waiting to fail. Water that builds up behind a wall creates hydrostatic pressure that can push even a well-built structure out of position over time. We assess the drainage conditions on your property before we design the wall, and we build a drainage solution into every retaining wall we install. That might mean a perforated pipe behind the wall, a gravel backfill layer, surface grading to redirect runoff, or a combination of all three. Getting the water management right is just as important as getting the wall right, and we don't skip that step.

    Footings Sized for the Load

    A wall is only as strong as what it sits on. We size footings based on wall height and the soil pressure it will carry. Shallow footings are a shortcut that shows up as a leaning wall a few years later. We do not take that shortcut.

    Concrete Retaining Walls for Heavy Loads

    Poured concrete retaining walls are one of the strongest options available for properties with significant grade changes or heavy soil pressure. We form and pour concrete walls with the right thickness, reinforcement, and batter to handle the load behind them. A concrete wall built with proper drainage will outlast nearly every other option on the market.

    Block and Segmental Retaining Wall Systems

    For projects where appearance matters as much as function, concrete block and segmental retaining wall systems are a strong option. These systems use interlocking units that create a clean, finished look while still providing serious structural support. They work well for tiered garden walls, landscaping borders, and residential applications where the wall will be visible from the street or backyard. We select block systems rated for the height and load requirements of your specific project, and we install geogrid reinforcement inside the wall when the height calls for it.

    Engineering-Backed Design

    SC Steel was founded by Tyler Partington, who brings an engineering background to every project. Taller walls and walls near structures or property lines require proper load calculations and design, not just experience. We bring that to every job.
