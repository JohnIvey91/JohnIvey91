    OUR STORY

    We started SC Steel & Concrete Professionals because we saw too many contractors cutting corners. With a background in engineering, we knew concrete work could be done better, and Shreveport deserved a company that actually proved it on every job.

    We built SC Steel by acquiring two established businesses: Surface Solutions Shreveport and Post Tension Foundation Services. That gave us the team, the knowledge, and the track record to handle everything from a backyard patio to a post-tension foundation repair. It's also where our 30+ years of combined experience comes from.

    Every slab we pour is reinforced with our SteelX system. It's not an upgrade or an add-on. It's standard on every project because it's the right way to build. That engineering-first approach is what sets us apart from contractors who are just pouring and moving on.

    We're not the biggest concrete company in Shreveport, and we're fine with that. We'd rather do fewer jobs the right way than rush through work that won't last. If that sounds like the kind of contractor you've been looking for, give us a call.

    FAITH, FAMILY, AND COMMUNITY

    When we're not on-site, you'll find us spending time with family, staying active in our church, and investing in the community that raised us. Those values aren't just personal. They shape how we run this business, how we treat our customers, and the standard we hold ourselves to on every job.

    Experience the difference of a contractor you can rely on. We put our name to it.
