Metal Building Concrete Contractor in Shreveport, LA

A metal building is only as good as the foundation it sits on. The slab has to be the right thickness, reinforced correctly, and — most importantly — poured with anchor bolts set to exact manufacturer specifications. If the bolts are off, the building does not go up right. If the slab is undersized for the load, it will crack. SC Steel & Concrete Professionals specializes in metal building concrete foundations built to spec and built for Louisiana soil conditions.

What Makes Metal Building Concrete Foundations Different

Pouring a slab for a metal building is not the same as pouring a standard residential slab. Metal buildings arrive with engineered drawings that specify exact anchor bolt locations, embedment depths, and slab dimensions. Those specs exist for a reason, and deviating from them causes real problems during erection and over the life of the structure.

The two most common failure points we see on metal building concrete foundations poured by crews without this experience are anchor bolts set in the wrong location and slabs that are not level within the required tolerance. Both of those mistakes create costly problems. A foundation done right the first time avoids all of it.

How We Approach the Work

We Work From the Building Plans

Every metal building foundation starts with the manufacturer's engineered drawings. We review the anchor bolt pattern, slab thickness requirements, and any special load provisions before we form or pour anything. If something in the drawings needs clarification, we get it resolved before work begins, not after the concrete is in the ground.

Precision Anchor Bolt Placement

Anchor bolts are set in the wet concrete and have to land exactly where the drawings call for. We use templates and measurement checks throughout the pour to make sure every bolt ends up in the right location at the right height. This is not a step that allows for much margin, and we treat it accordingly.

Slab Thickness and Reinforcement for the Load

A shop floor that will see regular vehicle traffic needs more thickness and reinforcement than a simple storage shed. We size every slab based on what the building will be used for and what loads it will carry. Every metal building foundation we pour includes our SteelX reinforcement system as a standard part of the job, which gives the slab the tensile strength to handle the stresses that come with Louisiana's soil movement over time.

Level to Manufacturer Tolerance

Metal buildings require a foundation that is level within a tight tolerance across the full footprint. We verify level throughout the pour and make corrections before the concrete sets. A slab that is off by even a small amount across a 40 or 60 foot span creates issues when the building crew arrives and tries to set the frame.
