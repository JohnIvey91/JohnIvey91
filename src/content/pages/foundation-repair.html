FOUNDATION REPAIR

Louisiana soil moves. The clay under your home swells when it rains and shrinks when it dries. That constant movement puts your foundation under stress year after year. If you are seeing cracks in your walls, doors that stick, or floors that no longer feel level, your foundation is telling you something is wrong. SC Steel & Concrete Professionals fixes the problem at the source, so your home stays stable for years to come. We also provide a full range of concrete services to protect and improve your property.

Warning Signs You Should Not Ignore

Most foundation problems start small and get worse over time. Catching them early saves you money and protects the value of your home. Watch for these signs:

  • Cracks in interior drywall, especially above doorways or at corners
  • Exterior brick or masonry cracks running in a stair-step pattern
  • Doors or windows that stick, jam, or no longer close properly
  • Gaps between walls and the ceiling or floor
  • Floors that slope, sag, or feel uneven underfoot

These signs do not fix themselves. The sooner you act, the less damage you will have to deal with.

Why Northwest Louisiana Foundations Fail

The soil in the Shreveport-Bossier City area is some of the most difficult in the country for foundations. Our expansive clay absorbs water and swells, then contracts as it dries out. This cycle repeats with every rain and every drought. Over time, that movement causes even well-built foundations to shift, crack, or settle unevenly.

Most contractors treat the symptom. We address the cause. Our team understands how local soil behaves across seasons, and we design every repair plan around the specific conditions on your property.
