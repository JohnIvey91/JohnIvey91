SC Steel & Concrete installs and repairs concrete driveways in Shreveport, Bossier City, and Northwest Louisiana. Every slab includes SteelX reinforcement as standard. Every repair starts with finding the cause first.

A properly installed concrete driveway lasts 20 to 30 years. Our SteelX reinforcement extends that lifespan by controlling cracking before it starts.

Why Driveways Fail in Louisiana

Louisiana's clay soil swells when wet and shrinks when dry, putting constant stress on any slab above it. A driveway poured without proper base prep or reinforcement will crack within a season or two. We see it all the time.

Repair or Replace?

Small cracks can often be repaired. Large cracks, sunken areas, or widespread crumbling usually mean replacement is the smarter investment. We'll tell you which makes sense for your situation.

Problems We Fix

  • Cracks and surface damage: We clean, fill, and seal to stop water intrusion. Patch or resurface depending on spread.
  • Sunken or settled areas: We lift sections back to grade or replace with proper base prep so it stays put.
  • Drainage issues: We evaluate water flow and fix the grade or add drainage to protect your foundation.

How We Build Driveways

  1. Site prep and base compaction. We excavate, remove unstable soil, and compact a proper base. This invisible step determines whether your slab stays flat.
  2. Sized for the load. Walkways and driveways carrying trucks don't need the same thickness. We size each slab to what it will carry.
  3. SteelX reinforcement. Every slab includes steel reinforcement. Not an upgrade, just how we build. Controls cracking and holds the slab together if soil shifts.
  4. Control joints. Concrete will crack. We cut joints at planned intervals so it cracks where we want it to.
  5. Proper curing. We use curing compounds and cover when needed. You won't use the slab until it's ready.
