Why Concrete Fails in Northwest Louisiana

Northwest Louisiana's clay soil swells when wet and contracts when dry, constantly shifting slabs above it. Heavy rainfall erodes base material, leaving voids that cause sections to sink or crack. We find the cause before we fix the surface. A patch on top of an unresolved problem will fail again.

Repair or Replace?

Repair makes sense when: cracks are isolated, settlement is limited to specific sections, the base is still sound, or the repair area is small.

Replacement makes sense when: cracking is widespread, the base has failed, settlement is severe throughout, or the concrete was poured too thin originally.

We'll walk you through this decision at the site visit.

What We Repair

  • Driveway cracks: We evaluate whether cracks are stable or actively widening, then use flexible fillers or address underlying support as needed.
  • Sunken concrete: We raise sunken sections back to grade using mudjacking or polyurethane foam injection. Most lifting projects are completed in a single day.
  • Surface damage: Spalling, pitting, and rough surfaces get resurfacing that restores appearance and durability at a fraction of replacement cost.
  • Sidewalks and walkways: We fix trip hazards and replace only damaged panels rather than the entire walkway.
  • Patios: Crack repair, settling fixes, and decorative overlays for extensive surface damage.
  • Expansion joints: Replacing deteriorated joint material protects slab edges and extends concrete life.

Why Choose Us

  • We address root causes, not just symptoms. Filling cracks without fixing why they formed guarantees they'll return.
  • Professional-grade materials designed for Louisiana's climate.
  • Color and texture matching so repairs blend with surrounding concrete.
  • Honest repair-vs-replace guidance. We won't push replacement when repairs will last.
