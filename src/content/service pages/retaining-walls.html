RETAINING WALLS

A slope that looks fine in the summer can turn into an erosion problem after a heavy Louisiana rain. Soil shifts. Yards wash out. Driveways crack at the edges. A properly built retaining wall stops that cycle before it causes real damage. SC Steel & Concrete Professionals designs and builds retaining walls that hold their ground through our climate, our soil, and whatever comes with them.

Do You Need a Retaining Wall?

Not every slope needs a wall, but some situations make one the right call. Here are the signs that a retaining wall could solve your problem:

  • Soil is washing down a slope after rain and collecting near your foundation or driveway
  • A yard or garden area on a slope is hard to maintain or losing topsoil
  • A hillside or raised area sits close to your home, driveway, or a neighboring property
  • You want to level out a sloped yard to add a patio, parking pad, or usable outdoor space
  • An existing wall is leaning, cracking, or showing signs of failure

If any of these sound familiar, the right wall in the right place can protect your property and open up space you are not using.

Why Louisiana Soil Makes This Harder Than It Looks

Retaining walls fail for a few common reasons: poor drainage, shallow footings, and no accounting for soil movement. In Northwest Louisiana, all three of those risks are higher than most places. Our expansive clay soil absorbs water, swells, and pushes against anything in its way. When it dries out, it contracts. That cycle puts pressure on a wall that was not built to handle it.

A wall that works fine in another part of the country can lean, bow, or collapse here in a few seasons. We build for Louisiana conditions from the start, not as an afterthought.

How SC Steel Builds Retaining Walls

Proper Drainage Is Not Optional

Water is the number one reason retaining walls fail. Without a way for water to escape, pressure builds behind the wall and pushes it forward. Every wall we build includes a drainage plan. That means gravel backfill, drainage pipe, and weep holes sized and placed based on the specific slope and soil on your property.

Footings Built for the Load

A wall is only as strong as what it sits on. We size footings based on the height of the wall and the soil pressure it will carry. Shallow footings are a shortcut that shows up as a leaning wall a few years later. We do not take that shortcut.

Material Options That Fit the Project

We work with several wall systems depending on what the project calls for. Segmental block systems like Versa-Lok and Redi-Rock are strong, flexible, and work well for most residential applications. Poured concrete walls are the right choice when you need maximum strength for taller walls or heavy loads. We will walk you through the options and recommend what makes sense for your situation.
