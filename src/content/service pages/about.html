Who We Are

Your home is one of your biggest investments. Choosing the right contractor matters.

At Steel & Concrete Professionals, we believe hiring a contractor should come with confidence, not uncertainty. Our goal is to deliver quality workmanship while making the construction process as transparent and organized as possible through clear communication and follow-through.

As a locally owned and operated company serving Shreveport, Bossier City, and the surrounding communities, we're proud to help homeowners improve and protect their homes. We specialize in driveways, patios, sidewalks, concrete pads, foundation repair, retaining walls, traditional and post-tension slabs, and other specialty concrete projects. While residential homeowners are our primary focus, we also partner with builders and contractors who rely on our expertise for specialized concrete work.

Steel & Concrete Professionals builds on the legacy of Surface Solutions and Post Tension Foundation Services, two respected North Louisiana companies that have served the region for more than 20 years. Today, we continue that legacy through quality workmanship with organized project management, dependable communication, and a commitment to continuous improvement.

Our Commitment

We believe quality workmanship is only part of a successful project. A great contractor also communicates proactively, sets clear expectations, keeps customers informed, and stands behind their work.

We're proud to be Better Business Bureau Accredited, and we encourage every customer to read our reviews, ask questions, and choose a contractor they feel confident trusting with their home or property.

Whether we're replacing a driveway, repairing a foundation, installing a retaining wall, or helping a builder complete a specialty concrete project, our commitment remains the same:

Deliver quality work. Communicate proactively. Treat people fairly. Never stop improving.
