Why Foundations Need Specialized Work

Metal buildings transfer load differently than wood-framed structures, and anchor bolt placement has to be precise. If the bolts are off by even a small margin, your building won't line up. We work directly from GEMCO's drawings to set anchor bolts at the exact spacing, depth, and projection required.

Foundation Types

  • Slab-On-Grade: Most common and economical. Concrete slab provides both foundation and floor. Best for garages, shops, and storage.
  • Perimeter Foundation with Gravel Floor: Concrete perimeter beam with gravel interior. Economical for storage buildings where a concrete floor isn't needed.
  • Pier Foundation: Concrete piers support the structure above grade. Used on slopes or where elevation prevents moisture issues.

Site Preparation

A slab is only as strong as what's underneath it. Shreveport's clay soil expands and contracts with moisture, causing slabs to crack if the base isn't prepared correctly. We grade and compact the subgrade, bring in crushed stone base, and install a moisture barrier before any concrete is poured.
