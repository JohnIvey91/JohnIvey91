WARNING SIGNS YOUR FOUNDATION NEEDS ATTENTION

The earlier you catch a foundation problem, the less the repair costs. Watch for:

  1. Cracks in drywall, especially above doorways or in corners
  2. Stair-step cracks in exterior brick or block
  3. Doors or windows that stick, drag, or no longer latch
  4. Gaps forming between walls and the ceiling or floor
  5. Floors that feel uneven, soft in spots, or slightly sloped
  6. New cracks in tile, along slab edges, or in your patio

None of these fix themselves. A quick inspection now can prevent a much larger repair bill later.
