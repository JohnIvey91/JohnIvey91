Warning Signs You Shouldn't Ignore

Foundation problems rarely fix themselves. What starts as a small crack in your drywall or a door that sticks can turn into a serious structural issue if left alone. Shreveport's clay-heavy soil expands and contracts with moisture, putting constant pressure on foundation walls and footings.

Ignoring foundation problems for even 6 months can double your repair costs. The earlier you catch a problem, the less it costs to fix. Watch for:

  • Cracks in drywall, especially above doorways or running diagonally from corners
  • Stair-step cracks in exterior brick or block
  • Doors or windows that stick, drag, or no longer latch
  • Gaps forming between walls and the ceiling or floor
  • Floors that feel uneven, soft in spots, or slightly sloped
  • New cracks in tile, along slab edges, or in your patio

None of these fix themselves. A quick inspection now can prevent a much larger repair bill later.

How We Repair Foundations

Every repair starts with understanding what caused the problem. We inspect inside and out, check drainage, and assess soil conditions. A surface fix on a deeper problem just fails again. We find the cause and fix that.

Pier and Underpinning Systems

Steel push piers and helical piers transfer your home's weight to stable soil deep below the surface. We pull permits, follow local codes, and document everything for your records.

Bowed Walls and Lateral Pressure

Bowed or leaning walls mean soil pressure is pushing against your foundation. We use wall anchors and carbon fiber straps to stop movement and reinforce from the inside, before cracks turn into failure.

Post-Tension Slab Specialists

Many local homes have post-tension slabs. Nicking a cable can compromise the entire foundation. Our team is trained to work on these systems correctly. Most contractors aren't.

Engineering Standards + SteelX Reinforcement

Founded by an engineer, we work to ACI standards, the same benchmarks used in commercial construction. Every repair includes our SteelX reinforcement system. Not an upgrade. Just how we build.
