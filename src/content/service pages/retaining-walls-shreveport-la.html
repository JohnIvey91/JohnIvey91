RETAINING WALLS

A slope that looks fine in summer can turn into an erosion problem after a heavy Louisiana rain. Soil shifts, yards wash out, driveways crack at the edges. A properly built retaining wall stops that cycle before it causes real damage. SC Steel & Concrete designs and builds retaining walls that hold through our climate, our soil, and whatever comes with them.

DO YOU NEED A RETAINING WALL?

Some situations make a wall the right call:

  • Soil washing down a slope and collecting near your foundation or driveway
  • A yard or garden on a slope losing topsoil and hard to maintain
  • A raised area sitting close to your home, driveway, or a neighboring property
  • You want to level a sloped yard to add a patio, parking pad, or usable outdoor space
  • An existing wall that is leaning, cracking, or showing signs of failure

WHY LOUISIANA SOIL MAKES THIS HARDER THAN IT LOOKS

Retaining walls fail for a few common reasons: poor drainage, shallow footings, and no accounting for soil movement. In Northwest Louisiana, all three risks are higher than most places. Our expansive clay soil absorbs water, swells, and pushes against anything in its way. When it dries out, it contracts. That cycle puts pressure on a wall that was not built to handle it. We build for Louisiana conditions from the start, not as an afterthought.

HOW WE BUILD

DRAINAGE FIRST

Water is the number one reason retaining walls fail. Without a way for water to escape, pressure builds behind the wall and pushes it forward. Every wall we build includes a drainage plan — gravel backfill, drainage pipe, and weep holes sized and placed based on the specific slope and soil on your property.

FOOTINGS SIZED FOR THE LOAD

A wall is only as strong as what it sits on. We size footings based on wall height and the soil pressure it will carry. Shallow footings are a shortcut that shows up as a leaning wall a few years later. We do not take that shortcut.

MATERIAL OPTIONS THAT FIT THE PROJECT

We work with several wall systems depending on what the project calls for. Segmental block systems like Versa-Lok and Redi-Rock are strong and work well for most residential applications. Poured concrete walls are the right choice when you need maximum strength for taller walls or heavy loads. We will recommend what makes sense for your situation.

ENGINEERING-BACKED DESIGN

SC Steel was founded by Tyler Partington, who brings an engineering background to every project. Taller walls and walls near structures or property lines require proper load calculations and design — not just experience. We bring that to every job.
