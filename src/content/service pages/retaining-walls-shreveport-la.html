When You Need a Retaining Wall

  • Hillside washing out during heavy rains
  • Slope causing erosion or drainage problems
  • Need level space for a patio, garden, or play area
  • Existing wall leaning or failing
  • Commercial slope stabilization or parking lot walls

Why Retaining Walls Fail

Water is the most common culprit. Without proper drainage, water builds behind the wall and creates pressure that pushes it out of position. Shreveport's clay soil makes it worse, expanding when wet and contracting when dry. Walls that aren't engineered for these forces will lean, crack, or collapse within a few years.

How We Build Retaining Walls

  1. Drainage planning. We build drainage into every wall: perforated pipe, gravel backfill, surface grading, or all three. Water management is just as important as the wall itself.
  2. Footings sized for the load. We size footings based on wall height and soil pressure. Shallow footings are a shortcut that shows up as a leaning wall later.
  3. Right wall system for the job. Poured concrete for heavy loads. Block systems for visible landscaping walls. We match the system to your specific requirements.
  4. Engineering-backed design. Tyler Partington brings an engineering background to every project. Taller walls and walls near structures require proper load calculations, not guesswork.
