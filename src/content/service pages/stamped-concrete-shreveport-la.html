Built for Shreveport's Climate

Louisiana weather is tough on outdoor surfaces. The heat, humidity, and heavy rain cycles cause plain concrete to crack and fade over time. We use a proper gravel base, the right concrete mix, and a high-quality sealer on every stamped concrete job we do. That sealer locks in the color and creates a protective barrier against moisture, UV rays, and everyday wear. We also cut control joints in the right spots to manage how the slab moves with temperature changes, which helps prevent cracking down the line.
