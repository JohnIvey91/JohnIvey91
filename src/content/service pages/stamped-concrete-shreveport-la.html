Built for Louisiana Weather

Heat, humidity, and heavy rain cause plain concrete to crack and fade. We use proper gravel base, the right concrete mix, and high-quality sealer on every job. The sealer locks in color and protects against moisture, UV rays, and everyday wear. We cut control joints in the right spots to manage slab movement and prevent cracking.
